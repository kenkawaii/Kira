FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to switch my accounting software? expand_more

No. Kira works alongside SQL Account, AutoCount, Xero, Bukku, Cynco, and others. We're the analytics layer on top.

Is my data safe? expand_more

Yes. PDPA-compliant, encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold or shared with third parties.

How accurate are the forecasts? expand_more

Honestly, it depends on your data. Most F&B businesses have enough sales history for useful weekly forecasts within their first month on Kira. We'll publish benchmarks once we have real customer data to back them up.

Do I need to be technical? expand_more

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Kira.

Is there a free trial? expand_more

Yes. Beta users get 6 months free in exchange for feedback and a few short interviews.

When can I get access? expand_more

Private beta opens in early 2026. Join the waitlist for priority access — we're prioritising Klang Valley, Penang, JB, and Kota Kinabalu first.

Do you support [my accounting software]? expand_more

CSV upload supports any system that exports a transaction file. Direct API integration is starting with SQL Account and AutoCount, with more on the roadmap.

How is Kira different from Cynco, Xero, or SQL Account? expand_more

Those tools record and report what already happened. Kira predicts what's going to happen next, with explanations. They cover bookkeeping. We cover decisions.
